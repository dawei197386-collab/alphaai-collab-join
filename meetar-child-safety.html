MEETAR / 美拉 兒童性剝削與性虐待（CSAE）防護標準

本頁說明 AlphaHK（由 WEI JIANWEN 以獨資形式在香港營運）如何在 MEETAR / 美拉（亦稱 MEETRA）中防止、發現、處理及舉報兒童性剝削與性虐待（Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation，下稱「CSAE」）相關內容與行為。

本應用面向成人社交與跨語言溝通，並非為兒童設計。Google Play 商店目標受眾為 18 歲或以上。我們禁止任何涉及未成年人的性剝削、性虐待、性化或誘騙行為。

1. 零容忍政策

  • 嚴禁上傳、傳送、分享、索取或儲存任何涉及未成年人的性內容、性化影像、色情或誘騙材料。
  • 嚴禁以任何方式對未成年人進行性接觸、性誘騙（grooming）、性剝削或威脅。
  • 違反者帳戶將被立即停用，相關內容會被移除，並在法律要求或合理必要時向主管機關舉報。

2. 年齡與產品設計

  • 商店與產品定位為 18 歲或以上用戶。
  • 私隱政策亦載明本應用並非為 13 歲以下兒童而設；實際商店目標年齡為 18+。
  • 我們不會特意收集兒童資料；若發現兒童帳戶或資料，會盡快刪除並採取限制措施。

3. 舉報與處理流程

  • 應用內舉報：用戶可透過應用內舉報／投訴功能標示不當訊息、個人檔案或行為。
  • 電郵舉報：亦可寫信至下方聯絡電郵，主旨請註明「Child safety / CSAE report」。
  • 我們會優先審核 CSAE 相關舉報，移除違規內容、限制或停用違規帳戶，並保留處理紀錄以供法律需要。
  • 若內容可能構成兒童性虐待材料（CSAM）或其他犯罪，我們會依適用法律向相關執法或兒童保護機構舉報。

4. 與執法及保護機構合作

  • 在法律要求或為保護兒童安全而有合理必要時，我們會配合合法查詢並提供相關資訊。
  • 香港用戶或舉報人亦可向本地執法機構求助；我們會在法律允許範圍內協助。

5. 員工與供應商

  • 僅有處理安全與舉報所需的必要人員可存取相關舉報資料。
  • 我們要求服務供應商在其服務範圍內採取合理的安全與濫用防護措施。

MEETAR / 美拉 Child Safety Standards (CSAE)

This page explains how AlphaHK (operated by WEI JIANWEN as a sole proprietor in Hong Kong) prevents, detects, responds to, and reports child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) related to MEETAR / 美拉 (also known as MEETRA).

The App is for adult social communication and cross-language connection. It is not designed for children. The Google Play target audience is 18 years of age and older. We prohibit any sexual exploitation, abuse, sexualization, or grooming of minors.

1. Zero-tolerance policy

  • Uploading, sending, sharing, soliciting, or storing any sexual content, sexualized imagery, pornography, or grooming material involving minors is strictly forbidden.
  • Any sexual contact, grooming, exploitation, or threats directed at minors are forbidden.
  • Violating accounts will be disabled, content removed, and where required or reasonably necessary we will report to competent authorities.

2. Age and product design

  • Store and product positioning target users 18+.
  • Our privacy policy states the App is not directed at children under 13; the store target audience is 18+.
  • We do not knowingly collect children’s data; if we find a child account or data, we delete it promptly and take restricting action.

3. Reporting and response

  • In-app reporting: users can flag abusive messages, profiles, or behavior through in-app report/complaint features.
  • Email reporting: write to the contact email below with subject “Child safety / CSAE report”.
  • We prioritize CSAE reports, remove violating content, restrict or disable accounts, and keep records as needed for legal obligations.
  • If content may constitute child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or another crime, we report it to law enforcement or child-protection authorities as required by applicable law.

4. Law enforcement and child-protection partners

  • When required by law or reasonably necessary to protect children, we cooperate with lawful requests and provide relevant information.
  • Users in Hong Kong may also contact local law enforcement; we assist within legal limits.

5. Staff and vendors

  • Only personnel who need access for safety and report handling may review related reports.
  • We expect service providers to apply reasonable security and abuse-prevention measures within their services.
