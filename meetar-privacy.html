MEETAR / 美拉 私隱政策

本政策說明 MEETAR / 美拉（亦稱 MEETRA，下稱「本應用」或「我們的服務」）如何收集、使用、分享及保護您的個人資料。本應用由香港的 AlphaHK（由個人經營者 WEI JIANWEN 以獨資形式營運，下稱「我們」）提供。

MEETAR 是一個「連結者社交中心」：協助不同語言背景的人建立和維繫關係。翻譯是本應用的一項能力，用來支援跨語言的溝通，而非唯一目的。

使用本應用即表示您已閱讀並理解本政策。如您不同意，請停止使用本應用。

1. 我們可能收集的資料

  • 帳戶資料：例如註冊或登入時提供的電郵地址、電話號碼、使用者名稱，或經第三方登入（如 Apple 登入）取得的基本識別資料。
  • 個人檔案：您選擇公開或填寫的暱稱、頭像、簡介、慣用語言、所在地區等。
  • 訊息內容：您發送和接收的文字及其他訊息內容，用於傳送訊息及提供翻譯功能。
  • 裝置資料：例如裝置型號、作業系統版本、應用程式版本、語言設定、時區、IP 地址、推送通知識別碼，以及當機記錄。
  • 使用數據：例如使用了哪些功能、使用時間及次數、錯誤記錄等，用於了解服務表現和改善產品。
  • 相機及相片（僅在您使用相關功能時）：例如掃描 QR 碼加好友、拍攝或選擇圖片作頭像或傳送。我們只會在您授權後存取，且只用於您主動啟動的功能。
  • 通訊錄（僅在您授權時）：如您選擇允許，本應用可存取通訊錄以協助您尋找或邀請朋友。您可隨時在裝置設定中撤回授權；不授權亦可正常使用其他功能。

我們不會刻意要求您提供敏感個人資料（例如健康、宗教或政治立場）。請避免在個人檔案中公開此類資料。

2. 我們如何使用資料

  • 提供和運作本應用的核心功能，包括帳戶、聊天、翻譯、加好友及其他社交功能；
  • 維護安全，例如防止詐騙、垃圾訊息、濫用、未經授權存取，以及處理舉報；
  • 排除故障、分析使用情況，以改善產品質素和使用體驗；
  • 與您聯絡，例如服務通知、安全提示或回覆您的查詢；
  • 遵守適用法律或回應合法要求。

3. 訊息及翻譯／人工智能處理

  • 當您使用翻譯或相關人工智能功能時，相關訊息內容會被傳送至我們的伺服器及／或翻譯或人工智能服務供應商進行處理，以便即時向您及對方提供譯文或相關結果。
  • 這些處理的目的是交付您所使用的功能。我們不會出售您的個人資料或訊息內容。
  • 我們不會將您的訊息內容用於廣告定向。
  • 我們會要求服務供應商只在提供服務所需的範圍內處理資料。由於不同供應商有其自身的資料處理條款，我們會盡力選擇提供適當保障的供應商。
  • 機器翻譯可能不完全準確，重要內容請自行核實。

4. 第三方及資料處理者

我們只會在以下情況與第三方分享資料：

  • 服務供應商：例如雲端主機及儲存、資料庫、翻譯及人工智能服務、推送通知、當機報告及數據分析服務。他們代表我們處理資料，以協助提供服務。
  • 其他用戶：您的個人檔案資料及您發送的訊息，會按功能設計向您選擇互動的用戶顯示。
  • 法律要求：在法律要求、法院命令或為保護用戶、公眾或我們的權利和安全而有合理必要時。
  • 業務轉移：如本應用的業務被轉讓，相關資料可能隨之轉移，並繼續受本政策或同等保障約束。

除上述情況外，我們不會出售、出租或交換您的個人資料。

5. 保留期限

  • 我們只會在提供服務所需及法律要求的期間內保留資料。
  • 帳戶資料一般於您的帳戶存續期間保留；當您刪除帳戶後，我們會在合理時間內刪除或匿名化相關資料，惟法律要求保留、處理糾紛或安全需要的除外。
  • 為提供翻譯而傳送給處理者的內容，只在完成處理所需的時間內使用；備份資料可能需要額外時間才完全清除。

6. 資料安全

我們採取合理的技術及管理措施保護資料，例如傳輸加密、存取控制及只限必要人員存取。然而，任何網絡傳輸或電子儲存方式都無法保證百分之百安全。請妥善保管您的帳戶及裝置。

7. 跨境資料轉移

我們位於香港。為提供服務，您的資料可能會在香港以外的地方（例如我們的雲端或翻譯／人工智能服務供應商所在的國家或地區）儲存或處理。這些地方的資料保護法律可能與您所在地不同。我們會採取合理步驟，確保您的資料在轉移後仍獲得適當保障。

8. 您的權利

視乎適用法律（包括香港《個人資料（私隱）條例》），您可以：

  • 查閱我們持有關於您的個人資料；
  • 更正不準確的資料（大部分個人檔案資料可在應用內直接修改）；
  • 刪除您的帳戶及相關資料——可透過應用內的刪除帳戶功能（如有提供），或電郵聯絡我們；
  • 撤回授權，例如在裝置設定中關閉相機、相片、通訊錄或通知權限；
  • 就資料處理提出查詢或投訴。

請透過下方第 11 節的電郵聯絡我們。為保障您的帳戶，我們可能需要核實您的身份。我們會在合理時間內回覆。

9. 兒童

本應用並非為 13 歲以下兒童而設，我們亦不會在知情的情況下收集 13 歲以下兒童的個人資料。如您所在地法律規定的最低年齡較高（例如歐盟部分地區為 16 歲），您必須達到該年齡才可使用本應用。如您認為有兒童向我們提供了個人資料，請聯絡我們，我們會盡快刪除。

10. 政策變更

我們可能不時更新本政策。更新後的版本會在本頁發佈，並更新頂部的生效日期。如有重大變更，我們會透過應用內通知或其他合理方式告知您。更新後繼續使用本應用，即表示您接受更新後的政策。

MEETAR / 美拉 Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MEETAR / 美拉 (also known as MEETRA; the "App" or our "Service") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data. The App is provided by AlphaHK, operated by WEI JIANWEN as a sole proprietor in Hong Kong ("we", "us", "our").

MEETAR is a connector social center that helps people from different language backgrounds build and keep relationships. Translation is a capability within the App that supports cross-language communication; it is not the only purpose.

By using the App, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App.

1. Data we may collect

  • Account information: such as the email address, phone number or username you provide when signing up or logging in, or basic identifiers received through a third-party sign-in (for example, Sign in with Apple).
  • Profile information: nickname, profile photo, bio, preferred languages, region, and other details you choose to add.
  • Message content: text and other content you send and receive, used to deliver messages and provide translation.
  • Device information: such as device model, operating system version, app version, language setting, time zone, IP address, push notification token, and crash logs.
  • Usage data: such as which features you use, when and how often, and error logs, used to understand performance and improve the product.
  • Camera and photos (only when you use related features): for example, scanning a QR code to add a friend, or taking or choosing an image for your profile or a message. We access these only after you grant permission, and only for the feature you start.
  • Contacts (only if you grant permission): if you allow it, the App may access your contacts to help you find or invite friends. You can withdraw this permission at any time in your device settings, and the rest of the App works without it.

We do not intentionally ask for sensitive personal data (such as health, religious or political views). Please avoid making such information public in your profile.

2. How we use data

  • To provide and run the App's core features, including accounts, chat, translation, adding friends and other social features;
  • To keep the Service safe, for example preventing fraud, spam, abuse and unauthorized access, and handling reports;
  • To fix problems, analyze usage, and improve product quality and user experience;
  • To contact you, for example with service notices, security alerts or replies to your questions;
  • To comply with applicable law or respond to lawful requests.

3. Messages and translation / AI processing

  • When you use translation or related AI features, the relevant message content is sent to our servers and/or translation or AI service providers for processing, so that the translation or result can be delivered to you and the people you talk with.
  • This processing is done to deliver the feature you are using. We do not sell your personal data or message content.
  • We do not use your message content for ad targeting.
  • We ask our service providers to process data only as needed to provide their service. Providers have their own data processing terms, and we aim to choose providers that offer appropriate safeguards.
  • Machine translation may not be fully accurate. Please check important content yourself.

4. Third parties and processors

We share data with third parties only in these cases:

  • Service providers: such as cloud hosting and storage, databases, translation and AI services, push notifications, crash reporting and analytics. They process data on our behalf to help provide the Service.
  • Other users: your profile information and the messages you send are shown to the users you choose to interact with, as the features are designed.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or court order, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights and safety of users, the public or us.
  • Business transfer: if the App's business is transferred, related data may be transferred too and will remain covered by this policy or equivalent protections.

Apart from the above, we do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

5. Retention

  • We keep data only as long as needed to provide the Service or as required by law.
  • Account data is generally kept while your account is active. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymize related data within a reasonable time, except where we must keep it for legal obligations, dispute resolution or security.
  • Content sent to processors for translation is used only for as long as needed to complete the processing. Backups may take additional time to be fully cleared.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as encryption in transit, access controls, and limiting access to those who need it. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. Please keep your account and device safe.

7. International transfers

We are based in Hong Kong. To provide the Service, your data may be stored or processed outside Hong Kong, for example in countries or regions where our cloud or translation/AI providers operate. Data protection laws there may differ from those where you live. We take reasonable steps to make sure your data stays appropriately protected after transfer.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance), you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate data (most profile details can be edited directly in the App);
  • Delete your account and related data, using the in-app account deletion option (where available) or by emailing us;
  • Withdraw permissions, for example turning off camera, photos, contacts or notification access in your device settings;
  • Ask questions or make a complaint about how your data is handled.

Please contact us using the email addresses in Section 11. To protect your account, we may need to verify your identity. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If the law where you live sets a higher minimum age (for example, 16 in some parts of the EU), you must meet that age to use the App. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date at the top. For significant changes, we will let you know through an in-app notice or other reasonable means. If you continue to use the App after an update, you accept the updated policy.
